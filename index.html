What I work on

I work across the device stack, from the first board bring-up to software that can ship, update, and keep running in the field.

Hardware integration

Sensors, power systems, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, Ultra-Wideband, LPWAN, and CAN—connected into a coherent product.

Device systems

Embedded Linux, Android services, hardware-facing applications, and the interfaces that make a whole device work together.

Production engineering

Safety, observability, automated testing, reproducible builds, reliable updates, and software designed for long-term ownership.

What has stayed constant

Autonomy, mastery, and purpose have shaped every chapter of my career—from the first connected products I shipped to the complex device software I help build today.

👨‍💻 Autonomy means taking ownership beyond the code: understanding the product, making sound trade-offs, and leaving a system healthier than I found it.

📖 Mastery is the habit of going one layer deeper. I am still driven by the same question that brought me to embedded systems in 2012: how does the whole thing really work?

🌟 Purpose is building technology that earns its place in the world—useful to people, dependable in practice, and developed with care.

About Me

Software engineer, product builder, and relentlessly curious generalist

I have been making software work with hardware since 2012. While studying computer science and earning an engineering degree in France, I spent six months in San Francisco with Spire, then joined the early team at Equisense before finishing school. That experience taught me what it takes to move from a prototype to physical products used in the field.

Since then, I have worked across low-power trackers, connected buildings, embedded blockchains, and World's Orb. Alongside that work, I continue to explore verifiable devices, Physical AI, and the Economy of Things through the open-source Vertices SDK.

Hardware becomes a product when software makes it dependable.
